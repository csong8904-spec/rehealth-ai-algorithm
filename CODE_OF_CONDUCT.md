# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what
is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language, personal or
political attacks, publishing private information, or conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Health-data rule

Never post personal health information, identifiable records, credentials, or private audit logs in an
issue, discussion, or pull request. Maintainers may immediately remove such material to protect people.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct
issues privately through the maintainer contact shown on the GitHub profile.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
